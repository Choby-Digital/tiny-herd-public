# Sorry to see you go 🐾

Tiny Herd has been uninstalled. Everything it stored lived on your device,
so it's already gone — nothing to delete on our end (there is no "our end").

**One question before you leave:** what made you uninstall?

Your answer genuinely shapes what gets built next — tell us via
[a quick GitHub issue](https://github.com/Choby-Digital/tiny-herd-public/issues/new?title=Why%20I%20uninstalled&labels=feedback)
or email [chobydev@gmail.com](mailto:chobydev@gmail.com).

Common fixes, in case one of these was the reason:

- **Pet in the way on a site you use a lot?** The popup has a
  "Hide pet here" button for exactly that — per-site, one click.
- **Too chatty?** Announcements, fun facts, and break reminders each have
  their own toggle.
- **Performance worries?** The pet pauses completely in background tabs and
  skips iframes; it's a single tiny canvas.
